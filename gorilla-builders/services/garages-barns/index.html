Why this matters in the northwest Valley

A garage or shop is a structure first. Slab thickness, footing depth, door heights and where the power comes from all have to be decided before the pour.

For an RV garage, the dimensions come from the vehicle: clear door height, door width, and the turning room to get in. Measure the rig, not the brochure.
