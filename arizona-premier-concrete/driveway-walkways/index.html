Driveways and walkways questions.

How long until I can drive on a new driveway?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. Cars and light trucks typically wait about a week. We give you dates for your job.

Will my driveway crack?

All concrete can crack. Properly placed joints control where it happens, and good base prep and curing cut down on the rest. We explain what to expect on the visit.

Can the finish be stamped or colored?

Yes. Stamped, stained and colored finishes are all available on driveways and walkways.

Do you need a permit to replace a driveway?

Often only when the work reaches the curb, apron or sidewalk, since that is public right of way. We tell you what your city needs.
