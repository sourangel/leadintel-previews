What's included.

Desert yards look dry until a monsoon storm drops an inch of rain in an hour. A wall that holds the hillside in July has to handle that water, so drainage and the footing matter as much as the face. We build practical walls to stabilize a property and decorative walls to add visual interest, and the design is yours to customize.

  • Practical retaining walls for slopes, grade changes and erosion control
  • Decorative walls that add visual interest to the yard
  • Walls that create level areas for patios, pools and driveways
  • Drainage planned for monsoon runoff
  • Custom designs and measurements from a site visit
  • High-quality materials and a finished wall that lasts

Retaining walls questions.

How tall can a retaining wall be without engineering?

That depends on your city. Taller walls, and walls that hold up a driveway, a building or a neighbor's grade, usually need engineering and a permit. We tell you what applies to your address.

Do retaining walls need drainage?

Yes. Water behind a wall adds pressure, and monsoon runoff adds a lot of it. Drainage is built into the design from the start.

Can the wall match my existing hardscape?

Usually. We can match colors and finishes on decorative walls, and we talk through options on the visit.

Can you replace a wall that is failing?

Yes. We assess why it is failing, since the cause is often drainage, and rebuild it so the same thing does not happen again.
