Why this matters in the Phoenix area

The hard part of an addition is where the new meets the old: foundations, framing and building systems have to tie into what is already there.

Zoning and permits come first. Setbacks, structural engineering and the home's style decide what can be added and where.

What is included

  • Guest casitas and in-law suites
  • Master suite extensions
  • Garage and workshop expansions
  • Structural engineering assessments and architectural coordination
  • Permits and zoning
  • Site preparation, foundation and framing
  • Tie-in to existing systems, then finishes

How it goes

  1. Planning and permits

    Structural assessment, architectural coordination, permits and zoning.

  2. Site preparation and foundation

    The ground is prepared and the new foundation is placed.

  3. Structure and systems

    Framing, and tying the new space into the existing home's systems.

  4. Finishes

    Interior and exterior finishes that match the original home, then a final walkthrough.

Is this the right fit?

  • You love the house and the location but need more room
  • You want a casita or in-law suite on the property
  • You want the addition to look original to the home
  • You want one builder from permit to paint
