What's included.

A pool deck has to drain away from the water, stay comfortable underfoot and hold up to chemicals and Arizona sun. Slope, joints and surface texture are planned before the pour. A refreshed deck can change how the whole backyard feels.

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Look at the deck

  2. 2

    Remove or prep

  3. 3

    Pour and finish

When to call us.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Your deck is faded, cracked or worn
  • You want to extend the pool patio
  • You want a stamped pattern around the pool
  • Water does not drain away from the deck
