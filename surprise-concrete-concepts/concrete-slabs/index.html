What's included.

Every slab depends on its size, thickness, finish and the base under it. We look at how much concrete the job needs, how a truck or pump can reach it and how much grading is required, and we use a boom truck or a standard concrete pump when the site calls for it.

  • Concrete slab pouring
  • Foundations
  • Shed and barn foundations
  • Dog runs and pads
  • Slabs for patios, walkways and sidewalks
  • Concrete pumping and boom truck access when the site needs it

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Measure and bid

    We check size, access and prep, then give you a bid.

  2. 2

    Grade and form

    The area is graded and forms are set to line and level.

  3. 3

    Place and finish

    Concrete is placed by chute, pump or boom truck, then finished.

  4. 4

    Cure

    We cure the slab and tell you when it can take a load.

When to call us.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • You need a pad for a shed, barn or dog run
  • You are adding a patio, sidewalk or foundation
  • A truck cannot get close to the pour area
  • You want a pour planned around access and prep

Slabs questions.

How thick is a typical slab?

It depends on the use. A patio and a driveway are different. We spec it for your job.

What if the truck cannot reach the spot?

We use a concrete pump or a boom truck to reach tight or far-away areas, and plan for it in the bid.

Can you pour a shed or barn foundation?

Yes. Shed and barn foundations are on our list of jobs.
