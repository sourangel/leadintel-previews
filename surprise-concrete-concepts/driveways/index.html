Concrete driveway repair and installation in Surprise, AZ

The driveway is the first thing you see when you come home. Surprise Concrete Concepts pours new driveways, replaces cracked and shifted ones, extends them for another car or RV, and offers stamped, polished, stained, colored or plain broom finishes.

What's included.

Cracks and heaving in Arizona driveways often trace back to tree roots, settling soil and years of heat. Fixing only the surface can bring the problem right back. Removing the cause, prepping the base and pouring with proper joints is what lasts. One review of ours describes just that: a root removed and a new driveway poured.

  • New driveway installation
  • Driveway replacement and crack repair
  • Driveway extensions for an extra car or small trailer
  • RV access to the side or back yard
  • Stamped, polished, acid-stained, colored or broom finishes
  • Free consultation and bid

Driveways questions.

Can a cracked driveway be repaired, or does it need replacing?

It depends on how wide the crack is, whether the slab has shifted, and what caused it. We look at it in person and tell you which makes sense.

Can you widen or extend my driveway?

Yes. We pour driveway extensions for an extra car or a small camping trailer, and RV access to the side or back yard.

What finishes are available?

Stamped, polished, acid stained, colored or a standard gray with a broom finish.
