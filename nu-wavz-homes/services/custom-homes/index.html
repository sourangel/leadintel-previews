Why this matters in the East Valley

Most of what decides how a custom build goes happens before the first shovel: the lot, the plan, the budget and who makes which decision when.

The owners, Dan Johnson and Ben Sivert, have over 60 years of building experience between them and stay in direct contact with each client.

How it goes

  1. Consultation

    You come into the office and we talk about your vision and budget. You leave with a design selection sheet and a projected budget to take to your lender.

  2. Design and floor plan

    You sit down with our designer, then we present a proposed floor plan and work on an elevation in the style you prefer.

  3. Contract and permits

    The contract fits your price point. We handle the permit paperwork while you finalize your selections.

  4. Construction and move in

    With plans approved, construction begins, and you can follow it in the online portal until the final walkthrough.

Questions about Custom Homes

Do I need to own land before I start?

Ideally yes. If you own land we can begin the feasibility and design process right away. If not, we can help evaluate lots to see whether they suit your build.

Can you design my home from scratch?

Yes. We work with you to create a fully custom floor plan around how you live.

How are changes handled during construction?

Changes are written up as change orders with updated pricing and timeline adjustments before work proceeds.
