How we build a custom home in Mesa, AZ

This is the order a custom home goes up with Nu Wavz Homes, from the first meeting to move-in day.

  • ROC #188086 / #293730
  • Licensed and insured in Arizona
  • Building since 2003
  • 4.6 stars from 33 reviews

  1. 1 Consultation and design

    You come into the office and we talk through your vision and budget. You get an overview of the whole process, a design selection sheet and a projected budget for your lender. Then you sit down with our designer and we present a proposed floor plan and elevation.

    What you decide: Bring your favorite floor plans and your vision for the home.

  2. 2 Contract and permits

    When it is time to sign, we provide actual calculations to fit your price point. Our contracts keep your budget on track. We handle the permit paperwork while you finish your plan details and product selections.

    What you decide: Approve the plan and the contract, and finalize your selections.

  3. 3 Dirt work

    The lot is cleared and graded to the plan so water drains away from the house.

    What you decide: Confirm the house location on the lot.

  4. 4 Footings and plumbing

    Forms and rebar go in square to the plan, and underground plumbing goes in before the slab. Each is inspected before concrete is placed.

    What you decide: Confirm fixture locations.

  5. 5 Slab

    The slab is placed and finished, then cured before framing starts.

    What you decide: Nothing. This is our job.

  6. 6 Framing

    Walls go up and the house takes shape. This is a good time to walk it and request changes.

    What you decide: Walk the framing and confirm room sizes and openings.

  7. 7 Roof

    Trusses and sheathing close the shell, then the roof covering goes on.

    What you decide: Confirm roof color and material.

  8. 8 Exterior

    Stucco, windows and doors go in, and rough-ins are inspected.

    What you decide: Confirm finishes and exterior colors.

  9. 9 Interior and finishes

    Drywall, cabinets, flooring, paint and fixtures go in, followed by the final inspection. The online portal lets you review selections and change orders at any hour.

    What you decide: Review your selections and walk it with us.

  10. 10 Move in

    Final walkthrough and move-in.

    What you decide: Walk it and move in.

Ask for the project schedule in planning. We provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase.
