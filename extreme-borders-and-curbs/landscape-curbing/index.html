Landscape curbing in Surprise, AZ

Landscape curbing is a concrete border that separates lawn from rock, planters from walkways and one part of the yard from the next. Extreme Borders and Curbs installs it for homeowners and commercial properties in Surprise and across the northwest Phoenix valley, as a lasting replacement for brick and wood edging.

What's included.

Rock beds and turf do not stay apart on their own. Decorative gravel drifts onto the lawn, Bermuda grass creeps into the rock, and edging that is not set into the ground shifts every season. A continuous concrete border holds the line and acts as a root barrier too. In valley heat and monsoon runoff, how the border is laid out, where its joints fall and how it cures decide how it looks in five years.

  • Concrete borders that replace costlier, less durable lawn edging like brick and wood
  • Flowing curves, straight runs and custom layouts
  • Residential and commercial properties
  • A choice of curbing styles, including the 6 by 4 inch square, round and mower profiles
  • A choice of stamped patterns, including Random Rock and Single Brick
  • A choice of colors from a sample board

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Lay out the line

    Walk the yard, decide where the border runs, and mark the curves and straight runs before any concrete goes in.

  2. 2

    Choose the look

    Pick the profile, the stamp pattern and the color from the samples.

  3. 3

    Form the border

    Prep the edge and place the concrete in a continuous run, shaped to the layout.

  4. 4

    Finish and cure

    Stamp and color the surface, cut the joints, and keep mowers and traffic off until it has cured.

Signs you need a border.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Rock or mulch spilling onto the lawn or the walkway
  • Grass creeping into gravel beds
  • Brick, wood or plastic edging that has shifted, rotted or cracked
  • Too much time spent trimming along the edges

Landscape curbing questions.

How long does concrete landscape curbing last?

Concrete is far more durable than wood, plastic or loose brick, which is why it replaces them. A long, thin border will still crack somewhere in Arizona heat, so clean joints and a sound layout matter. Ask how your border will be built.

Can landscape curbing follow curves?

Yes. Flowing curves, straight lines and custom shapes are the main reason people choose concrete over rigid edging.

Do you do commercial properties?

Yes. We serve both residential and commercial customers.

Do I need HOA approval for a border?

Many Surprise and West Valley communities review landscape changes. Check with your HOA first. The style, pattern and color samples help with the application.
