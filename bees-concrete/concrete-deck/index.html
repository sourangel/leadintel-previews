What's included.

Wood and composite decks fight the Arizona sun, and concrete simply does not rot or need regular staining. Concrete holds up to heavy foot traffic and extreme weather, and it can be stamped or stained to look like stone or wood, or left plain for a clean modern look. It is usually more affordable than wood or composite.

Concrete decks questions.

Why choose a concrete deck?

It is durable, low maintenance, can be customized and is usually more affordable than wood or composite.

Can it look like wood or stone?

Yes. Stamping and staining can mimic natural stone or wood.

Does it need sealing?

It does not need regular staining like wood, though sealing every few years is smart outdoors in Arizona.
