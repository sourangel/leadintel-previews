What's included.

Pouring the foundation is the last step of a big job for a home builder, and everything before it sets it up. Excavation, any old foundation removal, forms, the pour, backfill and cure each have to be done in order. Tucson soil can hide hard caliche or soft, expansive clay, and monsoon season can wet a site fast, so planning around both saves trouble.

  • Foundation installation for homes
  • Excavation around the building area
  • Removal of an existing foundation
  • Concrete poured into forms set in the excavation
  • Backfill around the new foundation
  • Curing before more construction
  • Free estimates

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Excavate

    The area is dug to the plan, and any existing foundation is removed.

  2. 2

    Form

    Forms are set in the excavation to the plan's lines and levels.

  3. 3

    Pour

    Concrete is placed into the forms, finished and protected.

  4. 4

    Backfill and cure

    The soil goes back around the foundation, and it cures before construction continues. Depending on weather, that wait runs about 24 hours to 7 days.

When to call.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • You are building a new home or addition
  • An existing foundation has to be removed and replaced
  • A lot is cleared and ready for excavation
  • A plan calls for footings and stem walls

Foundation installation questions.

What does foundation installation include?

Excavating, removing any old foundation, pouring concrete into forms, backfilling and letting it cure.

How long before building can continue?

Depending on the weather, the cure can take about 24 hours to 7 days. Your builder or engineer decides when framing can start.

Do I need engineered plans?

For a house or other structural foundation, yes, and a permit. The foundation is built to those plans.
