How a build goes

This is the order a new custom home goes up. Each stage is inspected before the next one starts.

  1. 1 Lot and plan

    We walk the lot, confirm the plan fits it, and agree on scope, budget and schedule in writing.

  2. 2 Dirt work

    The lot is cleared and graded to the plan so water drains away from the house.

  3. 3 Footings

    Forms and rebar go in square to the plan and are inspected before concrete is placed.

  4. 4 Plumbing

    Underground drain and supply lines go in before the slab, then are inspected.

  5. 5 Slab

    The slab is placed and finished, then cured before framing starts.

  6. 6 Framing

    Walls go up and the house takes shape. This is the best time to walk it and request changes.

  7. 7 Roof

    Trusses and sheathing close the shell, then the roof covering goes on.

  8. 8 Exterior

    Stucco, windows and doors go in. Mechanical, electrical and plumbing rough-ins are inspected.

  9. 9 Interior and finishes

    Drywall, cabinets, flooring, paint and fixtures, then a final inspection.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

New custom homes, home additions, remodels, repairs and rehabs, design services, energy efficiency work, and commercial remodels and tenant improvements.

Do you build new custom homes?

Yes. New homes are part of the work, alongside additions and remodels. Call and tell us about your lot and your plan.

Where do you work?

Raindance Builders is based in Goodyear and serves the Phoenix-Scottsdale area and beyond. If you are not sure we cover your town, call.

How do I check a contractor's license?

Ask for the license number and look it up with the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. Do this with any contractor you are considering.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope, permits and weather. Call and we will talk through what yours would involve.

Can I change things during the work?

Talk to us early about changes. Ask how change orders are handled on any job before you sign.
