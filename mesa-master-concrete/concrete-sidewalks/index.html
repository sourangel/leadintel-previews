What's included.

Walkways fail at the joints and edges: water gets under them, roots and soil move, and slabs trip people. Straight forms, a compacted base and joints in the right place keep a sidewalk flat and safe.

  • Concrete sidewalks and walkways
  • Durable, safe pathways that blend with your landscape
  • Precision installation and careful finishing

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Mark the route

    Walk the property and lay out where the walk goes and how wide it is.

  2. 2

    Prep and form

    Excavate, compact the base and set forms to a straight, level line.

  3. 3

    Pour and finish

    Place and finish the concrete with a safe surface.

  4. 4

    Cure and walk it

    Protect the new walk while it cures, then check it with you.

When to call us.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • A walk has cracked or lifted and is a tripping hazard
  • You have no path between the driveway and the door
  • You want a walkway around the house or yard
  • You are landscaping and need clean concrete edges

Can a sidewalk be curved?

Yes. Forms can be bent to a curve and the walk finished to match your landscape.

How wide should a walkway be?

That depends on use. We walk the site with you and recommend a width.

Yes, we do concrete repair. We will tell you whether a section needs replacing or repairing.
