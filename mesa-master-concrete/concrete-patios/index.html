What's included.

A patio looks simple, but the details show for years: level surfaces, drainage that sends water away from the house, and a finish that does not burn bare feet. Stamp patterns and colors are chosen on site against your home, so the patio looks like it belongs.

  • Stamped concrete patios that mimic natural stone, brick or wood
  • Concrete slab patios with clean lines, level surfaces and proper drainage
  • Concrete and artificial grass patios with segment, border, inlay, stepping stone or transition layouts
  • Colored and stained concrete patios
  • Sealed surfaces
  • Design through installation by one crew

Patios questions.

Can a patio look like stone, brick or wood?

Yes. Stamped concrete is textured and patterned to mimic natural stone, brick or wood, in a range of patterns and colors.

Can you combine concrete and artificial grass?

Yes. We build segment layouts, borders, inlays, stepping stones and transition zones. It gives you a solid, low-maintenance entertaining area.

Should a patio in the sun be a darker or lighter color?

Lighter colors stay cooler underfoot in Arizona summers. We can show you options and explain the tradeoff on your site.

Does a patio need sealing?

A sealed surface resists staining and wear and helps the color last. We seal stained and stamped patios and explain how often to reseal.
