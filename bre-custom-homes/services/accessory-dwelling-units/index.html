Accessory Dwelling Units in Surprise, AZ

An accessory dwelling unit is a second, smaller home on your lot, for family, guests or a place to work. BRE Custom Homes has built an accessory dwelling unit together with a custom home, permitted in 2022.

Why this matters in the West Valley

An ADU shares the lot with the main house, so utilities, setbacks and how the two buildings sit together matter from the first sketch.

Building the ADU with a new home can save duplicate site work compared with adding it later.

What is included

  • Lot and setback review
  • Plan coordination with the main home
  • Permits and inspections
  • Construction and finishes
  • A final walkthrough

How it goes

  1. Walk the lot

    We look at where a second building can go and what it needs.

  2. Plan

    The ADU is drawn to suit the lot and the main home.

  3. Build

    Site work through finishes, inspected at each stage.

  4. Finish

    Final walkthrough and a punch list we complete.

Is this the right fit?

  • You want space for family or guests on your own lot
  • You are planning a new custom home and want a second building with it
  • You own a lot with room for a second structure

Questions about Guest Houses and ADUs

Can an ADU be built at the same time as a custom home?

Often, yes, and it can save repeated site work. Call and tell us about the lot.

Do ADUs need a permit?

Yes, a permit and inspections are required. We handle that as part of the job.
