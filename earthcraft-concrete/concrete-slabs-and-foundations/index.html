What's included.

A foundation cannot be adjusted after it cures, so the work that matters happens before the truck arrives: soil and base prep, forms set to line and level, and steel and inspections where the plan calls for them. Valley soils can be hard in places and expansive in others, so the plans and the soil report set the details. We work to the drawings you have.

Slabs & Foundations questions.

Do I need a permit for a foundation?

Almost always. Foundations are structural, so cities inspect them. Ask your city, and tell us when you call so the schedule leaves room for inspections.

How long does a slab take to cure?

A slab is usually walkable within a day or two and keeps gaining strength for about 28 days. Your builder or engineer decides when framing can load it.

Can you pour a garage or garden shed slab?

Yes. We pour garage and garden shed slabs. Call with the size and location for a quote.
