Repair questions.

Can cracked concrete be repaired instead of replaced?

Often yes. Hairline cracks and chipped edges can be repaired. A panel that has settled or broken through may be better replaced. An assessment tells you which.

Why does concrete crack in the desert?

Heat, fast drying, shifting soil and missing or poorly placed joints can all cause cracks. Some cracking is normal, which is why joints are cut to guide it.

Do you repair commercial concrete?

Yes. Our commercial restoration work covers crack repair, resurfacing and reinforcement of driveways and sidewalks.
