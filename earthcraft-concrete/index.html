Questions we hear a lot.

How long until I can walk or drive on it?

Most slabs take foot traffic after about 24 to 48 hours. Cars and light trucks usually wait around seven days. Concrete keeps gaining strength for roughly 28 days, so ask for dates on your job.

Can you pour in Arizona summer heat?

Yes, with the right plan. In heat, pours are best started early, the mix and timing are adjusted, and the surface is kept from drying too fast.

What finishes do you offer?

Plain finished, stamped, stained and colored concrete, exposed aggregate, travertine style, concrete squares, Kool Deck, and concrete paired with artificial turf.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the city and the job. Foundations and work in the public right of way, such as a street apron or sidewalk, usually do. Check with your city or ask when you call.

How much does it cost?

It depends on area, thickness, prep, access and finish. Quotes are free and carry no obligation.

Should concrete be sealed?

For most driveways, patios and pool decks, yes. A sealer slows staining and wear. Ask what suits your finish and when to reapply.
