What's included.

Concrete suits the desert. It does not rot, warp or hold moisture, and it shrugs off heat that would cook other materials. The details are what you notice later: a slight slope away from the house, joints that line up with the layout, and a color or finish that does not turn hot or slick. Patios questions.

Is a plain concrete patio a good idea in Tempe?

Yes. Concrete holds up to desert heat, it does not rot or warp, and it is low maintenance. A plain slab can be dressed up later with rugs and furniture, or stained or stamped now.

Will a stamped patio get too hot to walk on?

Any dark surface in direct sun gets hot. Lighter colors and shade help. Tell us your sun exposure and we can talk through color choices.

Can I add turf to a concrete patio?

Yes. Concrete and artificial turf is a popular pairing. It adds green without watering and reduces the amount of hard surface.
