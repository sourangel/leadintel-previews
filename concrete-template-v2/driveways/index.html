What's included.

Most driveway failures start under the concrete. A base that was not compacted settles, water finds the gap, and the slab cracks along the weak line. Thickness, steel, joint spacing and finish timing all depend on what will drive on it and what your soil does, which is why we walk the site before we quote.

  • New concrete driveways and full tear-out and replacement
  • Grading and slope so water runs away from the house and garage
  • Compacted base and reinforcement sized to what will actually park on it
  • Control joints planned before the pour, not guessed afterward
  • Broom, smooth or stamped finishes, plus color and sealing
  • Aprons and tie-ins to the street or sidewalk where your city allows

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Walk the site

    We measure, check drainage and access, and note anything under the slab that has to be fixed first.

  2. 2

    Prep the base

    Excavate, compact the base, set forms to line and grade, and place steel on chairs.

  3. 3

    Pour and finish

    Place, screed and float on schedule. Finish for grip, then cut or tool the joints.

  4. 4

    Cure and seal

    Keep the surface from drying too fast, protect it from traffic, and seal when it is ready.

Signs it's time for a new driveway.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Cracks wider than a pencil line, or cracks that have shifted
  • Sections that have sunk or tilted toward the house
  • Standing water after rain
  • Surface flaking, pitting or crumbling at the edges

Driveways questions.

How thick should a driveway be?

Four inches is common for cars and light trucks. Heavier vehicles or soft soil call for more thickness, steel, or both. We size it to what will actually drive on it.

How long before I can drive on a new driveway?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. Cars and light trucks typically wait about seven days, and heavy vehicles longer. We give you dates for your pour.

Can you replace my old driveway instead of patching it?

Yes. We break out and haul away the old slab, fix the base, and pour new. If a repair makes more sense, we will say so.
