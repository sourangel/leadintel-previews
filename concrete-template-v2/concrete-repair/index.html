What's included.

A crack is a symptom. If the base is washing out or the slab has settled, patching the surface only hides the problem for a season. We look for the cause first, then repair what can be saved and replace what cannot, so you are not paying for the same fix twice.

  • Crack repair and joint sealing
  • Surface patching, resurfacing and stain restoration
  • Removal and replacement of failed sections
  • Full tear-out and re-pour where the base has failed
  • Fixing slopes that send water toward the house
  • Matching the finish of the existing concrete where we can

Signs it needs attention.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Cracks that keep opening or have a height difference
  • Sunken or tilted slabs
  • Flaking, pitting or scaling surface
  • Water that pools or runs toward the house

Concrete repair questions.

Can you fix a sunken slab?

Sometimes. It depends on why it sank. If the base has washed out, replacing the slab is usually the lasting fix. We tell you what we see and what we would do.

Will repaired concrete match the old concrete?

Patches rarely match perfectly because old concrete has weathered. We get as close as we can and tell you what to expect.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace?

Repair costs less up front when the slab is sound underneath. Replacement costs less over time when the base has failed. We quote both when it is close.
