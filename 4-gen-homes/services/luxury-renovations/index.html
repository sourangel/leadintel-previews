Why this matters in Phoenix, Scottsdale and Paradise Valley

The existing house sets the rules. 4 Gen Homes starts by investigating existing conditions and doing a structural assessment before it gives you an estimate.

Valley heat is hard on materials, so material selection is part of the process, not an afterthought.

What is included

  • Investigation of existing conditions
  • Structural assessment to see what can stay and what needs to come out
  • Conceptual design with architects and interior designers
  • Material selection chosen for Valley heat
  • Construction management with a detailed plan, timelines and estimates
  • Final walkthrough

How it goes

  1. Investigate

    A look at the layout, the location of the renovation and any obstructions.

  2. Assess

    A structural assessment to set the scope and an accurate estimate.

  3. Design

    Conceptual design with architects and interior designers, then material selection.

  4. Build

    Construction management and a final walkthrough.

Is this the right fit?

  • You own an established home in the Valley
  • Your budget starts at about $85,000
  • You want one team for design and construction
