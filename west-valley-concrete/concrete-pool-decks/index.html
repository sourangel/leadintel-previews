What's included.

A pool deck is bare feet, water and full sun. Surface temperature, grip when wet and how the deck handles heat all matter more here than on a driveway. We install options built for that, including Cool Deck, described as non-slip and comfortable when temperatures soar.

  • Stamped concrete pool decks that replicate natural stone or brick patterns
  • Finished concrete pool decks with sealers, dyes and flagstone-style designs
  • Travertine-style pool decks
  • Concrete squares pool decks
  • Stained concrete pool decks using acid-based stains
  • Cool Deck systems, a mix of sand, cement, acrylics and polymers
  • Above-ground and in-ground pools

When a new pool deck is worth it.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • The deck is too hot to walk on in summer
  • The surface is slippery, worn or stained
  • Cracks or chips are showing around the coping

Pool decks questions.

What is Cool Deck?

A pool deck surface made from a mix of sand, cement, acrylics and polymers. It is designed to be durable, non-slip and comfortable underfoot in heat.

What pool deck styles do you do?

Stamped, finished, travertine-style, concrete squares, stained and Cool Deck. We also resurface existing decks.

Is stained concrete a good fit around a pool?

Acid-based stains penetrate the surface and are built for weather. We help you pick colors and texture.

Can you resurface my existing pool deck?

Yes. See our concrete resurfacing page for pool deck resurfacing.
