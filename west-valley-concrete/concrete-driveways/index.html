What's included.

Most driveway trouble starts below the surface. Valley soil is dry and hard in summer and can move when monsoon water gets under a slab, so base prep, drainage and joint layout matter as much as the finish you see. Heat also shortens the working time on a pour, which is why the crew size and the start time matter.

  • Stamped concrete driveways in patterns that read as brick, slate or stone
  • Concrete slab driveways, from a standard 4 inch slab to more complex designs
  • Exposed aggregate driveways, with the top layer of stone left showing
  • Concrete and artificial grass driveways that combine both materials
  • Colored and stained finishes
  • Driveway resurfacing when the slab is sound but tired (see Concrete Resurfacing)

How we do it.

  1. 1

  2. 2

    Cure and use

    Let it cure before it takes weight. We tell you when your driveway is ready.

Signs it's time for a new or refinished driveway.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Cracks that have opened or shifted
  • Sections that have sunk or tilted
  • Water that sits after a monsoon storm
  • A surface that is flaking, faded or stained beyond cleaning

Driveways questions.

What driveway finishes do you offer?

Stamped, standard slab, exposed aggregate, colored and stained, and concrete with artificial grass strips. Tell us the look you want and we will show you the options.

Can you resurface a driveway instead of replacing it?

Often, yes, when the slab underneath is sound. We inspect it first and tell you which route fits. See our concrete resurfacing page.

How long before I can drive on it?

Concrete needs time to gain strength, and heat and mix change that. We give you a date for your pour, not a guess.

Do you serve my town?

Our main area is Glendale, and we work across most of Maricopa County, including Peoria, Surprise, Buckeye, Avondale, El Mirage and Sun City.
