Questions we hear a lot.

Which areas do you serve?

Our main service area is Glendale, AZ, and we provide concrete services to most of Maricopa County, including Sun City, Peoria, Buckeye, Avondale, El Mirage and Surprise.

Do you do residential and commercial work?

Yes. Both, from a garden shed slab to commercial foundations and footers.

Are quotes really free?

Yes. Quotes are free and done in person, with no obligation.

Can you pour in Arizona summer heat?

Concrete sets faster in heat, so summer pours are planned around temperature, mix and crew. Ask us how we would schedule yours.

Should concrete be sealed?

For most decorative surfaces, yes. A sealer helps protect color and slows staining. We will tell you what suits your finish.

What finishes can I choose?

Stamped, stained, colored, exposed aggregate, broom or smooth, concrete with artificial grass, plus pavers.
