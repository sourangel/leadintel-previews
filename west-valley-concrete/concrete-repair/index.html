What's included.

Most concrete damage grows. Water gets into a crack, heat expands the slab, and a small problem becomes a replacement. Repair makes sense when the base is sound, and we tell you plainly when it is not.

  • Repair of damaged concrete surfaces
  • Restoring surfaces to their original state
  • Resurfacing when repair alone will not hide the wear

Signs it needs repair.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Cracks, chips or crumbling edges
  • A trip edge between slabs
  • Pitted or flaking surface
  • Damage that is getting worse
