What's included.

Pavers only look as good as what is under them. A well-prepared, compacted base keeps joints tight and surfaces level, and material choice matters in desert heat: travertine, for example, stays cooler to the touch than many surfaces.

  • Travertine pavers, cool to the touch and suited to patios, pool decks and walkways
  • Concrete pavers for driveways, patios and pathways
  • Brick pavers for driveways, walkways and patios
  • Natural stone pavers, each with its own color and texture
  • Paver driveways, patios, pool decks, paths and walkways
  • Paver garden edging

Pavers questions.

What types of pavers do you install?

Travertine, concrete, brick and natural stone pavers.

Where can pavers go?

Driveways, patios, pool decks, paths and walkways, and as garden edging.

Are pavers good for pool decks?

Travertine pavers are slip-resistant and heat-friendly, which suits a pool area.
