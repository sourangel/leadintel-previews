Design-Build Custom Homes in Phoenix, AZ

Design-build means one company handles your design and your construction. SC Homes works that way, so the plan and the build are never at odds.

Plan your buildCall (480) 375-1011

Why this matters in the Phoenix and Scottsdale area

Splitting design and construction between separate firms is where many custom builds lose time and money. With one team, the person drawing the plan is thinking about how it gets built and what it costs.

In the Phoenix area, the lot shapes the home as much as the plan does: orientation to the sun, drainage, utilities and setbacks all matter, and it helps to have one team looking at all of them together.

What is included

  • Consultation about your lot, ideas and goals
  • Design of your home with the builder at the table
  • Construction from site work through finishes
  • A final walkthrough

How it goes

  1. Consultation

    We talk about your lot, your ideas and what you want from the process.

  2. Design

    The plan takes shape with building in mind, so decisions are practical as well as beautiful.

  3. Build

    Site work through finishes, one stage at a time.

  4. Move in

    A final walkthrough before you take the keys.

Is design-build the right fit?

  • You want one company accountable for both design and construction
  • You want to be involved in the decisions
  • You own a lot, or you are close to buying one
  • You plan to live in the home, and the details matter to you
