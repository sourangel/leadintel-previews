What's included.

Custom work is decided before it is poured. Shape, edges, joints, surface and how it will be used all affect how it holds up, and none of it can be changed after the concrete sets. In Yuma the sun and heat make planning matter more: a detailed finish needs working time, and hot weather shortens it. Talking through the plan first is how the finish ends up the way you pictured it.

  • Custom shapes and layouts worked out with you before the pour
  • Finish choices to talk through: broom, smooth, colored, stamped or other
  • Edges, borders and joints planned for the design
  • Sealing to protect the surface, as an option
  • Formed and poured to fit the space you have

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Describe the idea

    Tell the contractor what you want, where it goes and how it will be used.

  2. 2

    Plan the details

    Settle the shape, joints, finish and slope before any forms go in.

  3. 3

    Form and pour

    Build the forms to the plan, then place and finish the concrete while it is workable.

  4. 4

    Cure and protect

    Cure the surface slowly, then seal it if the finish calls for it.

When custom makes sense.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • You want a shape or finish that a plain slab does not give you
  • A space is an odd size or has curves or changes in level
  • You want the concrete to be part of the look of the yard
  • You are matching or upgrading an existing concrete area

Custom concrete questions.

What does custom concrete cost?

It depends on size, shape, prep and finish. The only reliable number comes from someone looking at the site and the design, so ask for a written quote.

Can a custom finish be added to an existing slab?

Sometimes. It depends on the condition of the slab and the finish you want. Ask when you describe the job.

Does sealing matter in the Yuma sun?

A sealer helps protect a decorative surface from sun, stains and wear. Ask what kind suits the finish and how often it needs redoing.
