What's included.

Prescott sits at about 5,400 feet. Winter nights drop below freezing, summer monsoon storms dump water fast, and the ground ranges from loose decomposed granite to pockets of clay. New concrete here has to shed water, handle freeze and thaw, and sit on a base that will not wash out. Most of the work happens before the truck arrives.

  • New pads for barns, sheds, shops and equipment
  • Flatwork for the house and yard, planned around drainage
  • Site and base prep, with forms set to line and level
  • Reinforcement chosen for what the slab will carry
  • Placing, screeding and finishing while the concrete is workable
  • Curing, so the surface does not dry too fast in sun and wind

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Talk it through

    What is it for, how heavy will it get, and where does the water go? Those answers set the thickness and the base.

  2. 2

    Prep and form

    Clear and shape the site, build a compacted base and set forms to the layout.

  3. 3

    Pour and finish

    Place the concrete, screed it level and finish it for the use, then cut or tool the joints.

  4. 4

    Cure and protect

    Keep the surface from drying too fast in the sun and wind, and from freezing in cold snaps.

When new concrete makes sense.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • You are building a barn, shop, shed or other outbuilding
  • You need a pad for equipment, a hot tub or a generator
  • There is gravel or dirt where you want a solid surface
  • You are adding on and need a slab that ties in cleanly

New concrete questions.

How long before I can use new concrete?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. Vehicles typically wait about a week, and concrete keeps gaining strength for around 28 days. Cold Prescott weather slows all of this.

Can you pour in winter at this elevation?

Concrete can be placed in cold weather with the right protection, because it must not freeze while it is still young. Nights below freezing can delay a pour. Ask about timing when you call.

Do I need a permit for a new slab?

It depends on the size, the use and whether anything will be built on it. The City of Prescott and the Town of Prescott Valley each have their own building departments, so check with yours or ask us.
