What's included.

Stamping is timing. The pattern has to go in while the surface is workable, and in Tucson summer heat that window is short. Color, release agent, stamps and sealer all have to be ready before the first truck arrives.

  • Stamped patios, walkways and entries
  • Stamped driveways
  • Pattern and color chosen before the pour
  • Border and joint layout planned to fit the pattern
  • Sealer applied after curing
  • Repair of stamped surfaces

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Choose pattern and color

    Pick the pattern and the colors, and decide where joints will fall.

  2. 2

    Prep and pour

    Base, forms and steel are done first, then the concrete is placed and leveled.

  3. 3

    Stamp and finish

    Apply color and release, press the pattern and finish the edges while the surface is ready.

  4. 4

    Cure and seal

    Cure, cut joints, wash off release and seal.

Stamped questions.

How long does stamped concrete last?

With a sound base and good sealer it can last for decades. The finish needs fresh sealer every few years in Arizona sun.

Does it crack?

All concrete can crack. Joints are planned to control where, and some are hidden in the pattern.

Can stamped concrete be repaired?

Small areas can often be patched, but color and pattern are hard to match exactly. Ask what a repair would look like.
