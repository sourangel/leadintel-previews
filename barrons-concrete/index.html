Questions we hear a lot.

How long until I can walk or drive on it?

Most slabs take foot traffic after about a day or two. Cars and light trucks usually wait around a week. Concrete keeps gaining strength for roughly 28 days, so ask for dates on your job.

Can you pour in Tucson summer heat?

Yes, with a plan. Big pours usually start early in the morning, and the surface has to be kept from drying too fast in 100 degree weather.

Do you do residential and commercial work?

Both. Barron's Concrete handles new construction, repairs and restorations, and occupied job sites.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the jurisdiction and the job. Foundations and structural work almost always do. Ask when you get your estimate.

How do you price a job?

By area, thickness, prep, access and finish. Estimates are free.

Should concrete be sealed?

For most patios, driveways and decorative surfaces, yes. A sealer slows staining and wear and needs to be reapplied from time to time in Arizona sun.
