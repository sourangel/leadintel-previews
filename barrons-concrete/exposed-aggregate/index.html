What's included.

The stone is revealed by washing away the surface paste at just the right time. Too early and the stone comes loose, too late and it will not expose. Heat speeds all of that up, so the crew and the schedule matter.

  • Exposed aggregate walkways, patios and driveways
  • Aggregate and color chosen from samples
  • Surface finish timed to the cure
  • Joints laid out to suit the surface
  • Sealer to bring out color and protect the surface
  • Slip-resistant surfaces around pools and entries

Aggregate questions.

Is exposed aggregate slippery?

It has more grip than a smooth trowel finish, which is why it is common around water. Ask about the aggregate size and finish.

Is it hot underfoot in the Arizona sun?

Any concrete in direct summer sun gets hot. Lighter colors stay cooler, so ask about color.

Does it need sealer?

Sealer protects the surface and deepens the color. It needs to be reapplied from time to time.
