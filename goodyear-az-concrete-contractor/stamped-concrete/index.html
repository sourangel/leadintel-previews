What's included.

A stamped slab only looks good if the pour, the color and the stamping all happen on schedule, because the surface has a short window when it takes a pattern. In the desert sun that window is shorter. After that, the sealer is what holds the color against UV and stains, and it needs refreshing every few years.

Stamped concrete questions.

How long does stamped concrete last?

A well-built stamped slab can last for many years. Resealing every few years keeps the color and pattern from fading in the sun.

Is stamped concrete slippery?

It can be when wet, so textures and sealers with grip are the better choice around pools and steps.

Can stamped concrete be repaired?

Cracks and worn spots can be repaired, but a patch rarely matches old colored concrete exactly because the original has weathered. A good installer will tell you what to expect.

How is stamped concrete different from plain concrete?

The base, steel and pour are the same. The difference is the color, the pattern pressed into the surface while it is workable, and the sealer on top.
