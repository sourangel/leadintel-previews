Stairs questions.

How high should each step be?

Residential codes usually limit riser height to about seven and three quarter inches, with treads at least ten inches deep. Every step in a run should match. Local code governs, so check yours.

Do concrete stairs need handrails?

Stairs with more than a few risers usually do under code, and the exact number depends on the jurisdiction. Plan for them before the pour.

Can old concrete steps be repaired?

Small chips and cracks can be patched. Steps that have settled or pulled away from the house are usually better replaced.

Can steps be colored or stamped?

Yes. Steps are often finished to match the patio or walkway they connect to.
