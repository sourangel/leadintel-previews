Questions to ask a custom home builder before you sign

A good builder will be glad you asked these. Use them with us or anyone else.

Licensing and insurance

Ask for the contractor license number and look it up with the state. In Arizona that is the Registrar of Contractors. Ask for a certificate of insurance and for the name of the carrier.

Contract and changes

Ask how change orders work, who approves them, and how allowances are tracked. Ask for the contract in advance and read it.

References and recent work

Ask to see homes finished in the last two years and to talk with those owners. Ask what went wrong and how it was fixed.

Warranty

Ask for the warranty in writing and ask who answers the phone when something needs fixing.
