Jon and Kim Albrecht.

  • ROC #279349

Kim and Jon Albrecht are the owners and operators of Prestwick Custom Homes. They have been building custom homes since 1988.

Jon is a third generation craftsman who started on custom home jobsites at 14, learning to read blueprints and working on everything from dirt to doorknobs. He graduated from Indiana University and became a CPA while working for construction companies, then formed Prestwick with Kim.

Kim has spent her career helping people find what suits them, from Pottery Barn to paint and granite choices. She goes with clients on selection trips and wants to hear them say "I love it" after every choice.

They live and raise their families in the East Valley, and say they support two local charities, AZCEND in Chandler and Feed My Starving Children in Mesa.
