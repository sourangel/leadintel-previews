Why this matters in the East Valley

A custom home is personal down to the small things: where the shower head sits, a shelf for one pair of boots, a light switch that is level. Prestwick says those details are what drive them.

They partner only with suppliers and craftsmen who share their approach. Those partners are East Valley based, which makes them more responsive and keeps showrooms convenient for homeowners.

What is included

  • Initial meeting to hear what matters to you
  • Planning, detailing and budgeting
  • Construction managed on site by the owner
  • Help with selections and finishes
  • Personalized details throughout the home

How it goes

  1. Meet and listen

Is this the right fit?

Questions about Custom Homes

Do I need an architect before I call?

No. Prestwick says you do not have to engage an architect first, and asks that you make them your first call.

Where do you build?

Custom homes and remodels in subdivisions, county islands and acreage across Gilbert, Chandler, Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Ahwatukee and Tempe.

Can I make changes during the build?

Their approach is hands-on management and flexibility, so changes can be made as the project comes to life.
