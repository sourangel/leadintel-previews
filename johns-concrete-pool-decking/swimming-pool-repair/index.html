What's included.

A pool surface lives in water, sun and chemistry all year, and in Tucson the sun and hard water are rough on it. Good repair work starts with an honest look at the shell and the surface, then the right fix: replaster when the finish is worn, repair when the problem is local, add features when you want them. Doing it in the right order keeps you from paying twice.

  • Pool replastering
  • Lagoon-style pool additions
  • Splash pad additions
  • Handrail installation
  • General pool repairs and improvements

Recent work.

Pool repair questions.

What is replastering?

It is putting a new interior surface on the pool shell when the old one is worn, rough or damaged. We can look at your pool and tell you if it is time.

Can you add a handrail to my pool?

Yes. Handrail installation is one of the services we offer, and it is a common safety upgrade at pool steps.

Can you add a splash pad or lagoon area?

Yes. Splash pad and lagoon-style additions are part of our pool work. We start by looking at your pool and yard and talking through what fits.

Do you fix the pool deck too?

Yes. Pool deck remodeling is our other main service, so the pool and deck can be done as one project.
