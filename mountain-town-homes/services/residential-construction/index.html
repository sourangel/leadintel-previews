Why this matters in Northern Arizona

Building in the mountains can be a difficult process. Snow load, frost depth, steep or forested lots and winter weather all shape how a house is designed and when each stage can happen.

The team says it has over 75 years of combined experience in Flagstaff. That is the combined experience of its people, not a claim about how long the company has existed.

What is included

  • Site clearing and development
  • Architects, draftsmen and project managers working on your plan
  • General contractors and craftsmen through construction
  • Mountain Modern design with rock and wood exteriors
  • Energy features such as tankless hot water, LED lighting, low-e windows and extra insulation
  • Final inspection

How it goes

  1. Consultation

    Start with a free discovery call about your lot, your plan and your goals.

  2. Design

    The design team turns what you want into a plan that suits the lot and the mountain climate.

  3. Build

    Site work through finishes, with each stage inspected before the next one starts.

  4. Final inspection

    A final inspection and a walkthrough before you move in.

Is this the right fit?

  • You want a home with a Mountain Modern look
  • You own a lot in Northern Arizona, or are close to buying one
  • You want one team from design to final inspection
  • You care about energy efficiency and monthly utility costs
