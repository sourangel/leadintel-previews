What's included.

Stain is translucent, so it shows the slab underneath: trowel marks, patches and old repairs all come through. Color also depends on the concrete, the weather and the sealer. A sample on your own slab, in your own light, is the only reliable way to know what you will get. Once a color goes down it is hard to change, so ask for a test patch first.

Stained concrete questions.

Will stain hide cracks and stains?

No. Stain is translucent, so cracks, patches and marks usually show through. Repair first and expect some of the history of the slab to remain visible.

Does the color fade in the sun?

Surface color on an outdoor slab fades over time in direct Arizona sun. A good sealer slows it, and resealing every few years keeps it fresh.

Can any concrete be stained?

Most can, but old sealers, curing compounds and oil stains can block it. The slab is tested before the whole area is colored.
