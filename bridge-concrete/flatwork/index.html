What's included.

In the Phoenix valley, flatwork takes sun, heat swings and the occasional monsoon downpour. A slab that is not graded sheds water toward the house or pools it at the low corner. Desert soil with a hard caliche layer can also hold water under a slab if the base is not prepared evenly. Getting the slope, the base and the joints right before the pour is what keeps a patio or driveway flat for years.

  • Patios, new builds and extensions to an existing patio
  • Driveways, driveway extensions and widening
  • Sidewalks and walkways, including extensions
  • Colored concrete patios
  • Grading so surfaces are safe and drain properly
  • Forms set to the layout, then placement and finishing

Signs it's time for new flatwork.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • You want a bigger driveway, a longer walk or a new patio
  • An old slab has cracked, sunk or tilted
  • Water collects on the surface or runs toward the house
  • You are adding a room or a garage and need the slab to match
