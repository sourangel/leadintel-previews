Questions we hear a lot.

How long until I can walk or drive on new concrete?

Most slabs take foot traffic after about 24 to 48 hours. Cars and light trucks usually wait around seven days. Concrete keeps gaining strength for roughly 28 days.

Can concrete be poured in Arizona summer heat?

Yes, with a plan. Hot weather speeds up setting, so good practice is early starts, the right mix and keeping the surface from drying too fast.

Which finish is best in the sun?

Lighter colors and textured finishes such as broom, exposed aggregate or Kool Deck stay more comfortable underfoot than smooth dark concrete.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the job and the city. Foundations and structural work almost always do. Ask before work starts.

How do I get a quote?

Should concrete be sealed?

For many driveways and patios, yes. A sealer slows staining and surface wear and helps protect color from sun.
