Slabs and foundations questions.

Do I need a permit for a foundation?

Almost always, yes. Foundations are structural, so cities inspect them. Ask what your job requires before work starts.

What is a stem wall foundation?

A stem wall is a short concrete wall that rises from the footing and supports the structure, often used on uneven or sloped ground.

How long does a slab take to cure?

A slab is usually walkable within a day or two and keeps gaining strength for about 28 days. Your builder or engineer decides when framing can load it.

Can you pour a pad for a garden shed?

Yes. Garage and garden shed slabs are part of the slab and pad work Scottscrete Concrete Contracting does.
