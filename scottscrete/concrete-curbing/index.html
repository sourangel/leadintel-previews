What's included.

Curbing holds gravel and soil in place in a desert landscape where there is little lawn to hold an edge. A smooth line, consistent height and a sound base keep it looking good for years and stop rock from migrating onto walks.

Curbing questions.

What is concrete curbing used for?

It borders beds, separates different areas of a yard and keeps rock and soil where they belong.

Can curbing be curved?

Yes. Curbing can follow straight lines or gentle curves around beds and walks.

Does curbing need to be reinforced?

That depends on length and use. Your contractor will tell you what suits your job.

Can it be colored?

Concrete curbing can be colored. Ask to see samples.
