Questions we hear a lot.

How long until I can walk or drive on new concrete?

Most slabs take foot traffic after about 24 to 48 hours. Cars and light trucks usually wait around seven days. Concrete keeps gaining strength for roughly 28 days, so ask for dates for your job.

Can concrete be poured in Arizona summer heat?

Yes, with a plan. In 100 degree weather concrete sets fast and the surface dries out quickly, so crews pour early, adjust the mix and keep the surface from drying too fast. Midday pours in July are where problems start.

How thick should a driveway be?

Four inches is common for cars and light trucks. Heavier vehicles or weak soil call for more thickness, steel, or both.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the city and the job. Foundations and structural work almost always do. Ask what your job needs before it starts.

What should I do before anyone digs?

Call Arizona 811 at least two full working days before digging so buried lines are marked. It is free and it is the law.

Should concrete be sealed?

For most driveways and patios, yes. A sealer slows staining and surface wear. Ask what suits your finish and when to reapply.
