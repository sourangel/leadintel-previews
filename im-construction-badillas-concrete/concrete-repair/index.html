What's included.

The cause decides the repair. A shrinkage crack can be filled and left alone. A slab that has sunk usually lost its base, and patching the top does nothing. Damaged surface needs removing back to sound concrete before anything new bonds to it. In the valley, summer heat and the monsoon both work on exposed concrete.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

Repair questions.

Can a crack be repaired, or does the slab need replacing?

A narrow, stable crack can be filled. A slab that keeps moving, or is crumbling throughout, usually needs replacing.

Will a repair match the old concrete?

Close, not perfect. Old concrete weathers, so a new patch stands out for a while.

Why does concrete crack in Arizona?

Shrinkage, heat, expansive soil and poor base prep. Most cracks are cosmetic. Cracks that shift are not.
