Stem wall patching in Goodyear, AZ

A stem wall is the low concrete wall at the base of many valley homes, under the stucco. I&M Construction advertises patching stem wall faces, including damage from rust, to make a home look cleaner.

What's included.

When steel sits too close to the surface it can rust, swell and pop the concrete face off, which is called spalling. The rust stain and broken edge are the sign. A good patch removes the loose concrete, deals with the exposed steel and rebuilds the face. Patching only the surface without dealing with the steel usually fails again.

  • Patching of stem wall faces
  • Repair of rust-related damage
  • Cleaning up broken and spalled edges
  • Matching the surrounding finish as closely as possible

Signs a stem wall needs patching.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Rust stains running down the wall
  • Chunks of concrete missing from the face
  • Exposed rebar or wire
  • Cracks along the base of the stucco

Stem walls questions.

What causes rust on a stem wall?

Steel too close to the surface, or water getting in, corrodes and swells the steel, which breaks off the concrete over it.

Is this a structural problem?

Often it is cosmetic. If a lot of steel is exposed or the wall is cracked through, have an engineer look at it.

Can it be done without touching the stucco?

Sometimes. It depends how far the damage reaches. Ask for a look at the wall first.
