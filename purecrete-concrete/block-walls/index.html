What's included.

A block wall is only as good as its footing. Valley block walls rest on a poured concrete footing with steel, and the cells are filled and grouted where the plan calls for it. Walls above a certain height, and any wall that holds back soil, usually need a permit and sometimes an engineer's design. The limit depends on the city.

  • Block yard and property walls
  • Block BBQ pits and outdoor features
  • Poured concrete footings
  • Steel and grout where the plan calls for it
  • Layout that follows the property line and your HOA rules
  • Coordination with the city where a permit applies

How we do it.

    Locate the line, call Arizona 811 before digging, and confirm height and permit rules.

  2. 2

  3. 3

Block walls questions.

Do I need a permit for a block wall?

Taller walls, and any wall holding back soil, usually do. The height limit varies by city, so check with Peoria.

Why do block walls crack?

Most often a footing that is too small or a base that moved. A proper footing and joints reduce it.

Can I put a wall on the property line?

Boundary walls involve your neighbor and sometimes the HOA. Confirm the line and the rules first.
