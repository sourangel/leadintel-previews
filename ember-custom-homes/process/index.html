How we build a custom home in Phoenix, AZ

Ember's design-build flow is consultation and planning, custom design, permitting and approvals, construction and a final walkthrough. This is what the construction stage looks like, from bare lot to move-in.

  • 25+ years of custom home experience
  • Design-build: one team, start to finish
  • Builds on your lot

  1. 1 Lot and plan

    We walk the lot, confirm the plan fits it, and settle scope, budget and schedule.

    What you decide: Sign off on the plan and the scope.

  2. 2 Dirt work

    The lot is cleared and graded to the plan so water drains away from the house.

    What you decide: Confirm the house location on the lot.

  3. 3 Footings

    Forms and rebar go in square to the plan and are inspected before concrete is placed.

    What you decide: Nothing. This is our job.

  4. 4 Plumbing

    Underground drain and supply lines go in before the slab, then are inspected.

    What you decide: Confirm fixture locations.

  5. 5 Slab

    The slab is placed and finished, then cured before framing starts.

    What you decide: Nothing. This is our job.

  6. 6 Framing

    Walls go up and the house takes shape. This is the best time to walk it and request changes.

    What you decide: Walk the framing and confirm room sizes and openings.

  7. 7 Roof

    Trusses and sheathing close the shell, then the roof covering goes on.

    What you decide: Confirm roof color and material.

  8. 8 Exterior

    Stucco, windows and doors go in. Mechanical, electrical and plumbing rough-ins are inspected.

    What you decide: Confirm finishes and exterior colors.

  9. 9 Interior and finishes

    Drywall, cabinets, flooring, paint and fixtures, then a final inspection.

    What you decide: Select finishes, then walk it with us.

  10. 10 Move in

    Final walkthrough and a punch list we finish, then you move in.

    What you decide: Walk it, give us the punch list and move in.

Timelines are typical ranges for this region and depend on size, permits and weather.
