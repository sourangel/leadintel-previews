Why this matters in the Phoenix metro area

Most problems on a custom build come from handoffs: the designer, the builder and the trades each working from a different version of the plan. A single design-build team keeps one version and one point of accountability.

Good project management is the quiet part: sequencing the trades, holding quality checks at each phase and keeping you informed so schedule and budget stay in view. The founder stays personally involved in every project.

What is included

  • Construction of your home by a seasoned build team
  • Project management to keep the work on schedule and on budget
  • Quality control at every phase
  • Work coordinated with the design team, from groundbreaking to final walkthrough
  • Clear breakdowns of costs and open communication at every stage
  • A final walkthrough when the home is done

How it goes

  1. Plan and price

    We settle the plan and the scope and break down the costs.

  2. Permits

    We manage permitting and approvals before any work starts.

  3. Build

    Site work through finishes, with the same team that designed it.

  4. Walkthrough

    We walk the finished home with you before you move in.

Is this the right fit?

  • You want one team accountable for design and construction
  • You want to hear about problems from the builder, not find them
  • You are building on your own lot
  • You want clear cost breakdowns at each stage

Questions about Construction

What does design-build mean?

One team handles design, permitting and construction together, instead of you coordinating an architect and a separate builder.

Can you build on my lot?

Yes. Ember Custom Homes builds on your lot or preferred location across the Phoenix metro area, from Paradise Valley to Scottsdale and beyond.

How will I know where the project stands?

Open communication at every stage is part of how we work, backed by hands-on project management.
