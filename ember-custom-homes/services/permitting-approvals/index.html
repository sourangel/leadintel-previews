Home Permitting and Approvals in Phoenix, AZ

Permits and approvals can be the hardest part of building a custom home. Ember Custom Homes handles city and county permitting, inspections and approvals for you, so the paperwork does not become your second job.

Why this matters in the Phoenix metro area

Every city and county in the Phoenix metro area has its own process, and hillside, desert-preserve and HOA-governed lots can add design review on top of the building permit. Missing one step costs weeks.

Ember Custom Homes handles zoning research, building permit applications and code compliance across Phoenix and surrounding communities, and coordinates with local authorities and inspectors to avoid delays.

What is included

  • Zoning research for your lot
  • Building permit applications, including plans and site preparation
  • Code compliance across Phoenix and surrounding communities
  • Coordination with city and county authorities and inspectors
  • Inspections scheduled and managed on your behalf
  • A smoother path to the start of construction

How it goes

  1. Research

    We check zoning and what your lot and neighborhood require.

  2. Apply

    We prepare and submit the plans, permits and site documents.

  3. Coordinate

    We work with the city, county and inspectors to keep it moving.

  4. Approve

    Approvals in hand, construction can start with nothing outstanding.

Signs you want this handled for you.

  • You have never pulled a permit for a new home
  • Your lot is in a hillside area or an HOA with design review
  • You want one team accountable for the paperwork and the build
  • You would rather not spend your weekdays at a permit counter

Questions about Permitting

Do I have to pull permits myself?

No. Ember Custom Homes manages permitting and approvals on your behalf as part of the design-build process.

Which cities do you permit in?

Phoenix and the surrounding communities in the Phoenix metro area. Tell us your lot's address and we will tell you what applies.

Does an HOA add approvals?

Often. Many communities have architectural review in addition to the city permit. We tell you what your lot needs in the consultation.
