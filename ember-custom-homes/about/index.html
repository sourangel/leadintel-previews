Ember Custom Homes is a luxury residential design-build firm serving the the Phoenix metro area. It takes a hands-on, boutique approach: the personal attention of a small firm, with the ability to carry out large custom estate projects.

Brett Randall founded the company to make custom homebuilding more personal, transparent and rewarding. Brett has more than 25 years of experience building custom homes in the Phoenix metro area, and he leads a team of architects, designers and craftsmen. He stays personally involved in every project.

The company's values are integrity, craftsmanship and communication: doing what you say you will do, taking pride in fine details and quality materials, and keeping clients informed from start to finish.
