Why this matters in Southern Arizona

Bathrooms are small rooms with a lot of trades: plumbing, tile, electrical, ventilation and cabinetry. The order they work in decides how smoothly it goes.

Water is the thing to plan around. Waterproofing behind tile and ventilation are the parts you never see and always depend on.

What is included

  • New vanities, showers and tubs
  • Flooring
  • Energy-efficient fixtures
  • Added storage
  • Lighting and ventilation
  • A finished look that matches the rest of the house

How it goes

  1. Look at the room

    What you use, what bothers you and what is behind the walls.

  2. Plan

    Layout, fixtures and finishes chosen and written down.

  3. Build

    Demolition, plumbing and electrical, waterproofing, tile and fixtures.

  4. Finish

    A walkthrough and a punch list we complete.

Is this the right fit?

  • A shower or tub is worn out or leaks
  • You want more storage or a different layout
  • You want to replace flooring and fixtures together
  • You would rather deal with one contractor than several trades
