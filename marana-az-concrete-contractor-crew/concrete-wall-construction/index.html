What's included.

Insulated concrete form walls hold concrete between layers of foam, so the wall carries load and insulates at once. In a desert cooling climate that cuts heat gain, and the solid core adds fire resistance, sound dampening and security. It all starts with a sound foundation.

  • ICF and insulated foam block wall construction
  • Simple walls or more detailed designs
  • Built to a height that suits security and privacy needs
  • Solid footing and foundation work under the wall
  • Residential and commercial projects

Walls questions.

What is ICF construction?

Insulated concrete forms are foam blocks stacked like building blocks and filled with concrete. The foam stays in place as insulation.

Does it help with energy bills?

Insulated walls slow heat transfer and air leaks, which helps in a hot climate. We do not promise a number.

Are concrete walls fire resistant?

Concrete does not burn and does not release toxic gas, so it resists the spread of fire.

Do walls need a permit?

Often, yes. We tell you what your wall needs in Marana during the estimate.
