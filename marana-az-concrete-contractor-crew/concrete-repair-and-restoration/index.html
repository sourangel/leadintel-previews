What's included.

Arizona concrete takes sun, heat swings and monsoon water, and small cracks let water in and widen. Repair early and a patch is cheap. Wait, and slabs sink, trip people and cost far more. We look at what is wrong first, then pick the right repair.

  • Assessment of cracks, potholes and worn areas
  • Patching and crack repair
  • Resurfacing and staining for a uniform look
  • Repairs on pools, driveways and patios
  • Residential and commercial surfaces

Repair questions.

Can cracked concrete be repaired?

Often, yes. Many cracks and worn areas can be patched or resurfaced without a full replacement.

Is repair cheaper than replacing?

Usually, when the slab is still sound. We tell you honestly if replacement is the better call.

Why repair early?

Water gets in through small cracks and makes them worse. Early repair costs less.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We assess the project and break down the costs before you decide.
