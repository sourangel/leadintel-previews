What's included.

In Marana the driveway fights sun, monsoon runoff and the desert's hard, dry soil. Good prep, proper drainage and a finish suited to 110 degree days decide whether a drive stays flat or cracks in the first few summers. Stamped and exposed aggregate finishes are both on the table.

  • Removal of gravel, dirt or failing paving
  • Grading and base prep for the load
  • Concrete placed for heavy use and resistance to cracking
  • Stamped concrete or exposed aggregate finishes
  • Residential and commercial driveways
  • Advice on cleaning and resealing

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Site visit

    We look at the slope, access and what the driveway has to carry, then give you a free estimate.

  2. 2

    Remove and grade

    Old gravel, dirt or broken concrete comes out, and the base is graded so water runs where it should.

  3. 3

    Form and pour

    Forms are set, concrete is placed and finished, with the finish you chose.

  4. 4

    Cure and walk through

    We let it cure properly, then walk it with you and explain sealing.

Signs you need a new driveway.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Gravel or dirt that washes out and ruts
  • Potholes, cracks or sunken, uneven slabs
  • Tripping hazards where slabs have shifted
  • A drive that no longer suits the house

Driveways questions.

How long does a concrete driveway last?

With good prep and routine care it lasts for many years. Sweeping, washing and occasional resealing protect the surface.

Can the driveway have a decorative finish?

Yes. Stamped concrete and exposed aggregate are both options.

Do you do commercial driveways?

We serve residential and commercial properties. Call with the details for a free estimate.

Should I seal my driveway?

Usually yes. A sealer slows stains from oil and weathering. We explain when to reapply.
