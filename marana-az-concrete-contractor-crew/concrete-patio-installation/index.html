What's included.

Arizona patios live outdoors nearly year round, so shade, surface temperature and drainage matter as much as looks. Concrete takes heat and traffic better than wood and does not need replacing piece by piece. Stamped patterns, borders and color can echo stone, brick or tile.

Patios questions.

Is concrete better than pavers for a patio?

Concrete is durable, needs no piece-by-piece replacement and takes heat and foot traffic well. Pavers have their place, and we will tell you plainly which suits your yard.

Can a patio look like stone or brick?

Yes, stamped patterns and borders can resemble natural stone, brick or tile.

Will a patio add value to my home?

Outdoor living space is something many buyers like. We cannot promise a figure, only a well-built slab.

How long before I can use it?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. We give you dates for your job.
