What's included.

Refinishing puts a new surface on sound concrete. It is a good choice when the slab is solid but looks old, stained or plain. Options include water based stain, acid stain, acrylic overlays and acrylic faux flagstone, plus resealing worn patios and pool decks. The concrete underneath has to be sound, so the first step is always a close look at the slab.

  • Concrete refinishing to renew or restore the look of a surface
  • Staining, including water based and acid stain
  • Acrylic overlays and acrylic faux flagstone
  • Re-staining and re-sealing worn patios and pool decks

Refinishing questions.

What is the difference between refinishing and repair?

Repair fixes damage. Refinishing renews the look of concrete that is structurally fine. Many jobs involve both.

Can refinishing change the color?

Yes. Stains and overlays can give existing concrete a new color and texture.

What is acrylic faux flagstone?

It is an acrylic surface applied over concrete and textured to look like flagstone, with joints in a contrasting color.
