What's included.

Professional driveway installation means planning the pour, placing the concrete, and making sure it is surfaced and level as it cures. In Scottsdale and Phoenix the details that decide how a driveway ages are the base under it, how water leaves it, and how it handles a Phoenix summer. Repair starts with finding out why a driveway cracked or went out of level.

  • Concrete driveway installation, planned and poured as one job
  • Surfacing and leveling as the concrete cures
  • Driveway repair for damaged, unlevel or unsightly slabs
  • An assessment of your driveway and a quote on repair options

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Look at the site

    Check size, slope, access and what the old driveway is doing, if there is one.

  2. 2

    Prep and form

    Prepare the base, set forms to line and grade, and plan the joints before the truck arrives.

  3. 3

    Place the concrete, screed it level and finish it while it is workable.

  4. 4

    Cure

    Keep the surface from drying too fast, a real concern in Arizona heat, and keep vehicles off until it is ready.

Signs a driveway needs attention.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Cracks that keep opening or have a step between the two sides
  • A section that has sunk or tilted
  • A surface that is flaking, stained or looks tired
  • Water that runs toward the garage or house instead of away

Driveways questions.

Can a damaged driveway be repaired instead of replaced?

Sometimes. The business will come assess the driveway and quote the proper repair options. If the damage comes from the base failing, replacement is usually the lasting fix.

Why do driveways crack in the Phoenix valley?

Fast drying in heat, a base that settled, and slabs without planned joints are the usual causes. Joints give the concrete a controlled place to crack.

How long before I can drive on a new driveway?

Foot traffic is usually fine within a day or two. Vehicles wait longer, and heavy ones longer still. Ask for dates that fit your pour.
