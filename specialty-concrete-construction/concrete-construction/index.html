What's included.

A finish only looks as good as the slab under it, and in the Phoenix valley the finish has to handle sun, heat and the occasional monsoon downpour. Staining and sealing work best on sound, clean concrete. Stamped and colored work depends on timing during the pour. Exposed aggregate and acid stain each behave differently in strong sun, so the method is chosen for the surface and the location.

  • Concrete flooring, surfaced in a range of textures or polished smooth
  • Repair by resurfacing and refinishing, or re-pour and refinish when the damage is deep
  • Sealing to extend the life of the concrete, especially etched or stained surfaces
  • Staining on old or new concrete, including acid stain
  • Stamped concrete that imitates stone, tile, brick or wood, in a range of patterns and colors
  • Colored concrete, exposed aggregate and acrylic overlays with engraved or saw-cut texture

Concrete construction questions.

Can old concrete be stained or colored?

Yes. Staining can be applied to old as well as new concrete. The surface has to be clean and sound first, and the result depends on the concrete underneath.

What is acid stain?

Acid stain reacts chemically with the concrete and highlights its natural color variations, so every floor comes out a little different.

Is exposed aggregate a good choice outside?

It is a durable, skid-resistant finish for outdoor areas, and it comes in a range of colors, stone sizes and hardness.

Does decorative concrete need sealing?

Usually yes. A sealer helps the color last and keeps the surface easier to clean, and it matters most on stained or etched concrete.
