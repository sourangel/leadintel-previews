Signs your sidewalk needs work.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Sections lifted into a trip edge
  • Cracks that have opened or shifted
  • Standing water that does not drain
  • A worn, slippery or stained surface

Sidewalks questions.

Can a sidewalk be decorative?

Yes. Options include decorative overlays, acid staining, epoxy coatings and stenciling.

Do I need a permit for a sidewalk?

Walks on your own property usually do not. Anything in the public right of way, or tying into a street or curb, usually does. Check with your city.

How soon can I walk on a new sidewalk?

Most new concrete takes foot traffic after a day or two. Ask for the date on your job.
