What's included.

Plain concrete gets very hot in an Arizona summer, and a wet deck can be slick. That is why finish and surface treatment matter as much as the pour: a textured, non-slip finish, a color that handles the sun, and for many owners a cool-deck topping. Cracks and uneven sections also need attention early, because water getting under a deck makes them worse.

  • Stamped concrete pool decks in a range of patterns, colors and textures
  • Plain finished concrete pool decks
  • Concrete squares pool decks laid in a grid, with optional different colors per square
  • Stained concrete pool decks, acid-based stains or water-based dyes
  • Kool Decking, a topping that makes the deck more comfortable underfoot and adds slip resistance
  • Pool deck resurfacing: crack repair, slip-resistant coatings or a new design

Signs your pool deck needs attention.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • The deck is too hot to walk on in summer
  • The surface is slick or worn smooth
  • Cracks, or sections that have shifted
  • Stains or fading that cleaning will not remove

Pool decks questions.

What is Kool Decking?

It is a topping applied over a concrete pool deck. It is meant to keep the surface more comfortable underfoot in the sun and to add grip. We can quote it on an existing deck.

Can my existing pool deck be resurfaced?

Yes, in many cases. Resurfacing can fix cracks, add slip-resistant coatings or give the deck a new design, as long as the deck underneath is sound.

What pool deck styles do you install?

Stamped, plain finished, concrete squares and stained. Call for a free quote and we can talk through which suits your pool.
