Acrylic concrete coating and resurfacing in Avondale, AZ

When a slab is sound but looks worn out, the answer is often a new surface, not a new slab. Sun Valley Decorative Concrete LLC applies acrylic coatings to patios, decks and walkways across the West Valley and greater Phoenix to renew the look and protect the concrete from the elements.

What's included.

A coating hides surface wear, not structural problems. If the slab is sound, a coating can make it look new and add protection from sun and water. If the base has settled or is moving, we tell you that first. In the Arizona sun, the color and finish are a big part of the choice, because dark surfaces hold heat.

  • Acrylic coatings for patios, pool decks, walkways and other concrete
  • Resurfacing of worn or faded slabs
  • Cleaning and repair of the surface before coating
  • Color and texture options
  • Protection against sun and weather
  • A free, no-obligation quote

How we do it.

  1. 1

    Look at the surface

    We check the slab, the wear and what is on the surface now.

  2. 2

    Clean and repair

    The surface is cleaned and any cracks or rough spots are repaired.

  3. 3

    Coat and texture

    The acrylic goes on in the color and texture you picked.

  4. 4

    Cure and walk through

    We let it cure and walk the finished job with you.

When a coating makes sense.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • A patio or deck that is sound but faded, worn or stained
  • You want a new look without removing the concrete
  • A surface that holds too much heat in the afternoon
  • A previous coating that has peeled or worn through

Acrylic coating questions.

Is an acrylic coating the same as paint?

They are different. Coatings are made for concrete and traffic, and the prep is more thorough than for paint.

Can you put it over my existing patio?

Often, if the slab is sound. We check first and tell you what needs to be fixed.

How long will it last in the sun?

It depends on the product, the traffic and how well the surface was prepared. We tell you what to expect and when to look at it again.

Do you coat decks and pool surrounds?

Yes. Customers have had decks, patios and pool decks resurfaced.
