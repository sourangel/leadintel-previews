Questions we hear a lot.

Do you pour new concrete?

Our work is on concrete that is already there: staining, coating, sealing, epoxy and Kool Deck. If your slab needs to come out, we will tell you.

How long before I can walk on it?

It depends on the product. We tell you when foot traffic is fine and when furniture, cars or weight can go back.

Can you work in Arizona summer heat?

Heat changes how coatings and sealers behave, so timing matters. We plan the work around the temperature of the surface and tell you the schedule.

Do you work on commercial jobs?

Yes, residential and commercial. Tell us about the space and we will tell you what makes sense.

How do you price a job?

By area, surface condition, prep and the product you choose. The quote is free, and we explain what is driving the number.

Should my concrete be sealed?

For most patios and walkways, yes. A sealer slows staining and surface wear. We tell you what suits your finish and when to redo it.
