What's included.

Stain soaks into the concrete, so the slab underneath shows through: its texture, its trowel marks and its flaws. That is the look, and also the limit. Old coatings, oil and sealers can block stain, so the surface has to be cleaned and tested first. We talk about what your slab will and will not take before you pick a color.

  • Stain on patios, walkways, floors and other interior and exterior concrete
  • Surface cleaning and prep, including removing what blocks stain
  • Stain that enhances the natural texture of the concrete
  • Color options, from soft earth tones to bolder choices
  • Sealer over the stain to protect it
  • A free, no-obligation quote

Concrete stain questions.

Will stain hide cracks and stains in my slab?

Stain is translucent, so it does not hide them. It can blend them in, and cracks may be repaired first. We tell you what to expect before we start.

Can you stain a slab that was sealed before?

Maybe. Old sealers have to come off first, and we test a spot to see how the slab responds.

Does stain fade in the Arizona sun?

Sunlight wears on every outdoor finish. A good sealer slows it, and we explain how often to renew it.

Is stain different from paint or a coating?

Yes. Stain soaks in and colors the concrete itself. A coating sits on top as a film.
