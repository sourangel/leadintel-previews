What's included.

Sealer wears away. In Phoenix, UV breaks it down, and a stamped patio or exposed aggregate that has lost its sealer fades, stains and dusts. Resealing before the surface fails is far cheaper than repair. It also brings back the color on stamped and exposed aggregate surfaces.

  • UV protection against Arizona sun

  • Enhances color on stamped and exposed aggregate

Signs it needs resealing.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Water soaks into the surface instead of beading
  • A stamped or colored surface that is fading and looks dull
  • Oil and tire stains that will not come out
  • A driveway or patio that has never been sealed

Concrete reseal questions.

How often should concrete be resealed?

It depends on the sealer and how much sun and traffic the surface gets. In Arizona sun, plan on resealing every few years, and sooner if water stops beading.

What is the difference between penetrating and film-forming sealer?

Penetrating sealers soak in and protect from within without changing the look much. Film-forming sealers sit on top and can add sheen and color enhancement.

Can sealer be applied to new concrete?

Yes, after it has cured enough. Sealing new stamped and decorative concrete is part of protecting the color.
