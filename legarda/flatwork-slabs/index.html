What's included.

Most flatwork problems start before the concrete arrives. Phoenix-area soil is often hard caliche that drains poorly or fill that settles, and a slab on a loose or uneven base will crack along the weak spot. In July the mix can start to set before the crew finishes, so timing, pour hours and curing matter as much as the mix. Every job we price begins with a site visit to look at the ground, the drainage and the access.

Flatwork & slabs questions.

How thick should a driveway slab be?

Four inches is common for cars and light trucks. Heavier vehicles, soft ground or equipment call for more thickness, steel, or both. We size it to what will sit on it.

Is it a problem to pour concrete in summer?

It takes planning. In Arizona heat concrete sets fast and can dry too quickly at the surface, so crews pour early, finish quickly and keep the slab from drying out too fast while it cures.

When can I walk or drive on it?

Most slabs take foot traffic after a day or two. Cars usually wait about a week, and concrete keeps gaining strength for roughly 28 days. We give you dates for your job.

Why does the slab have joints cut or tooled into it?

Concrete shrinks as it cures. Control joints give that shrinking a planned place to crack, so the cracks stay straight and tight instead of wandering across the slab.
