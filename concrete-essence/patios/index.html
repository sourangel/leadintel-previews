What's included.

A patio needs the right slope, a good base and a finish that does not burn bare feet. Water has to run away from the house, not toward it, and a patio that is poured against a slab or wall needs joints so it can move without cracking. Dark, smooth concrete holds a lot of heat in July, so the finish and color you pick matter as much as the pour.

  • Concrete patios for residential and commercial properties
  • Layout, forms and a slope that drains away from the building
  • Base prep and compaction
  • Control and isolation joints where the patio meets the house
  • A broom or other finish chosen for grip and heat
  • Curing and sealing advice

Patios questions.

How soon can I use a new patio?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. Wait about a week before heavy furniture, a grill or a hot tub, and longer for anything very heavy.

Will a concrete patio crack?

Concrete shrinks as it cures, so joints are cut to give it planned places to crack. A tight crack along a joint is normal. Random cracks usually trace back to base prep or joint spacing.

Is concrete too hot to walk on in summer?

Dark, smooth concrete can get very hot in the sun. A broom finish and a lighter color help, and a sealer or shade can too.
