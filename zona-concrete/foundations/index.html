Foundations questions.

What does the foundation process look like?

Dirt is imported, the forms are set, the pad is graded, rebar is placed and finally the concrete is poured.

How long before we can keep building?

Zona says concrete foundations take about 4 to 6 weeks to dry before the rest of the building process continues. Your builder and inspector decide when to load it, so ask them for dates.

Why choose a concrete foundation floor?

It is easy to clean and maintain. You can add an integral color of your choice and have exposed concrete floors throughout the house.

What is the difference between a foundation and a slab?

Zona's answer: a foundation is the main living space of the house by square footage. Slabs are the outdoor living spaces, such as patios.
