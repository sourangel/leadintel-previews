What's included.

The finish is the part you see and feel every day. Zona's advice is simple: exposed aggregate for driveways to avoid seeing tire marks, sand and sweat finishes for patios, and smooth hard trowel for foundations. Finish and color are decided before the pour, because they cannot be added afterward, and Zona does not do overlays, coatings or staining after the fact.

  • Smooth hard trowel
  • Sweat finish
  • Sand finish
  • Exposed aggregate
  • Stamped concrete
  • Your choice of color added to the mix

Textures & Finishes questions.

Which finishes do you offer?

Smooth hard trowel, sweat finish, sand finish, exposed aggregate and stamped concrete. Sand, exposed, sweat and salt finishes are also named for driveways.

Which do you recommend where?

Exposed aggregate for driveways, sand and sweat finish for patios, and smooth hard trowel for foundations.

Can I get colors other than gray?

Yes. Your color of choice is added to the concrete mix.

Do you stain or coat existing concrete?

No. Concrete overlays, repairs, coatings and staining are outside what Zona offers, and they recommend Hastings Coatings.
