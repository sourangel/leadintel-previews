Questions we hear a lot.

How do I choose a concrete contractor?

Zona suggests checking the Arizona Registrar of Contractors for licenses and the Better Business Bureau for reviews.

Which finish do you recommend?

Exposed aggregate for driveways, sand and sweat finishes for patios, and smooth hard trowel for foundations.

Are there colors other than concrete gray?

Yes. Your color of choice can be added to the concrete mix.

What is the process for a driveway?

Demolition of the old driveway, forming, compacting the dirt and pouring the concrete. Zona says it takes about 24 hours to dry.

Do you do overlays, repairs or coatings?

No. Zona focuses on complete design and concrete pouring. For overlays, repairs, coatings and staining they recommend Hastings Coatings.

Why concrete instead of asphalt?

A concrete driveway never needs to be recoated. Asphalt needs recoating about every two years.
