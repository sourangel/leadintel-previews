What's included.

Patios and walks sit in full sun and get used barefoot, so finish and color count more than they do on a driveway. For patios Zona suggests a sand finish or a sweat finish, which both look good and read as natural stone. Joints, slope away from the house and a good base keep a patio flat through the summers.

  • Poured concrete patios, small and large
  • Walkways, stepping pads and curved paths
  • Sand, sweat, exposed aggregate and stamped finishes
  • Color added to the mix
  • Fire pit surrounds and stone-faced edges and seat walls
  • Design and project management with Mac

Patios & Hardscapes questions.

Which finish suits a patio?

Zona recommends sand and sweat finishes for patios. Exposed aggregate and stamped concrete are also available.

Can the concrete be colored?

Yes. The color goes into the mix, so it runs all the way through. Zona links a Davis Colors chart on its site for picking colors.

How big a patio do you do?

A reviewer on Google describes a team of 8 working on more than 1,000 square feet of patios. Zona also takes smaller homeowner projects.

Do you do overlays or coatings on old patios?

No. Zona pours new concrete and does not do overlays, repairs or coatings. For those jobs they recommend Hastings Coatings.
