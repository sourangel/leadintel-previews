What's included.

Pool decks take water, chemicals and direct sun, and everyone walks on them barefoot. Surface texture, color and sealer matter more here than on almost any other slab, and the deck has to drain away from the pool and the house.

  • Stamped concrete pool decks
  • Finished concrete pool decks with sealer, staining and scoring
  • Travertine style pool decks
  • Concrete squares pool decks
  • Colored concrete pool decks
  • Kool Deck systems, a slip-resistant surface that stays cooler underfoot

When a new pool deck makes sense.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • The surface is cracked, stained or too rough on bare feet
  • Water stands on the deck or runs toward the house
  • The deck gets too hot to walk on
  • You are updating the pool and want the deck to match

Our work.

Pool decks questions.

What is a Kool Deck?

Kool Deck is a surface system made for pool decks. It is slip-resistant and stays cooler underfoot than plain concrete, and it comes in several colors.

Is stamped concrete slippery around a pool?

It depends on the texture and sealer. A textured stamp pattern and the right sealer give good grip. We can talk through the options for your deck.

Can you resurface my existing pool deck?

Yes. We resurface pool decks with overlays and coatings. See our concrete resurfacing page.

What colors work best in Arizona sun?

Lighter tones tend to stay cooler on bare feet. Color choices are large, so ask to see samples.
