What's included.

A slab cannot be adjusted after it cures, so the work that matters happens before the truck arrives: soil and base prep, forms set to line and level, steel where the plan calls for it, and enough crew to place and finish while the concrete is workable. On a hot day in the West Valley that window is short. We work to your plans, and your city decides which inspections the job needs.

Slabs questions.

Do you pour foundation slabs?

Yes. Foundations are one of the slab jobs we list, for homes and commercial projects.

Can you work from my engineer's plans?

Yes. A slab is built to the drawings. If something on site does not match the plan, flag it before the pour, not after.

Do I need a permit for a slab?

Foundations and structural slabs almost always need one. Your city decides which inspections apply, and we tell you what your job needs.

How long does a slab take to cure?

A slab is usually walkable within a day or two and keeps gaining strength for about 28 days. Your builder or engineer decides when framing can load it.
