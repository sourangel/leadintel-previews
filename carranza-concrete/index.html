Questions we hear a lot.

What kind of concrete work do you do?

Driveways, patios, slabs, sidewalks and commercial projects, for homes and businesses in the Phoenix valley.

Is your estimate free?

Yes. We ask you to send the estimate form, call you, then meet in person to take measurements and talk through the project.

How long until I can walk or drive on it?

Most slabs take foot traffic after about 24 to 48 hours. Cars and light trucks usually wait around seven days. Concrete keeps gaining strength for roughly 28 days, so we give you dates for your job.

Can you pour in the Arizona summer?

Yes, with a plan. Heat speeds up how fast concrete sets, so the mix, the crew size and the pour time all have to account for it, and the surface has to be kept from drying too fast.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the city and the job. Foundations and structural work almost always do, and so does a sidewalk in the public right of way. We tell you what applies to yours.

Should concrete be sealed?

For most driveways and patios, a sealer slows staining and surface wear. We talk through what suits your finish at the estimate.
