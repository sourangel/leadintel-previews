What's included.

Summer in the West Valley is hard on a driveway. Pavement surface temperatures run well past 140 degrees, a slab that dries too fast on a hot day crazes and cracks, and the desert soil under it can be loose or hard caliche that does not drain evenly. That is why the base, the joints and the timing of the finish matter more here than they do in a mild climate. We walk the site before we quote, so the price reflects what is under the slab as well as what is on top.

  • New concrete driveways for homes and commercial properties
  • Driveway repair and full tear-out and replacement
  • Broom, stamped and exposed aggregate finishes
  • Base prepared and forms set to line and grade before the pour
  • Control joints planned for the layout
  • In-person estimate with measurements, and a design if you want one

Signs it's time to repair or replace.

If any of these sound familiar, the sooner someone looks at it the cheaper it usually is to fix.

  • Cracks that have widened or shifted so one side sits higher
  • Sections that have sunk or tilted
  • Water that pools on the slab or runs toward the garage
  • Flaking, pitting or a surface worn smooth at the edges

Driveways questions.

Do you do stamped and exposed aggregate driveways?

Yes. We pour broom, stamped and exposed aggregate finishes. Tell us which look you want at the estimate and we will talk through color and pattern.

Can you replace my existing driveway?

Yes. We install, repair and replace driveways. If a repair is enough, we say so at the estimate.

How long before I can drive on a new driveway?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. Cars and light trucks typically wait about seven days, and heavy vehicles longer. In summer heat the timing can shift, so we give you dates for your pour.

Do you work on commercial driveways too?

Yes. We install driveways for residential and commercial properties.
