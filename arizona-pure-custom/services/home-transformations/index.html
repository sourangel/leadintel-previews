Why this matters in the East Valley

A large transformation is closer to a custom build than a repair: it needs a plan, permits and a schedule, and it has the added challenge of an existing structure.

Starting from your current home means the lot, the location and the neighbors are already settled. The project is about making the house itself right.

What is included

  • A walkthrough of your current home and what you want from it
  • Design with the same architect relationships used for new homes
  • Permits and approvals handled on your behalf
  • Construction managed with the same attention to detail as a new build
  • Final inspections and a walkthrough of what changed

How it goes

  1. Consultation

    We look at the home and talk through what you want to change.

  2. Design

    The changes are drawn and reviewed against the existing structure.

  3. Permits

    Approvals are submitted and followed.

  4. Build

    Construction, final inspections and a walkthrough.

Is this the right fit?

  • You like your location but not your floor plan
  • The project is large enough to need design and permits
  • You want a builder with custom home experience
  • You would rather improve than move
