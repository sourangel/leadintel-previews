Meet with Alex

It starts with the land.

We build on your lot, so the pad is cut and compacted to your plan before anything else goes in. Buckeye, Goodyear, Surprise, Estrella Mountain Ranch, Rainbow Valley.

Everything stands on this.

Forms, rebar and footings go in square to the plan. Alex takes his clients’ calls himself and welcomes a walk-through at any stage.

Before the concrete, the plumbing.

Drain lines and soft water loops go in under the slab first. Soft water loops come standard on every model.

The slab.

Everything above it depends on it.

Walls go up.

2×6 exterior walls insulated with 5½ inches of cellulose, and ceilings you choose: 10 feet, 12 feet or vaulted.

Then the roof.

Trusses and sheathing close the shell. R-38 cellulose insulation goes into the ceiling.

Tile, in your color.

Color-thru roof tile in the color you choose, set in the Arizona sun.

Stucco, glass, and a door worth walking through.

Synthetic stucco, dual-pane low-E windows and a custom 3′6″ × 8′ front door. The cabinets come from our own shop, Designer’s Choice Custom Cabinets.

Room for the whole lot.

The house is only part of it. Bring Alex your ideas for the yard, the patio and everything outside the door.

Your lot is next.

Meetings with Alex Carroll are by appointment. Call to set one up.
